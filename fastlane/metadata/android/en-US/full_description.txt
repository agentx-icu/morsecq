Learn Morse code at your own pace without an account. MorseCQ keeps learning history, settings, materials and recordings on your device.

Start with dit/dah and K/M, then move through guided recognition, sending practice and independent copying. Koch-method lessons, per-character progress, assessment and spaced review support continued practice. Assisted exercises are kept separate from independent mastery.

Features include:
• Guided lessons, recognition drills and touch or physical-keyboard sending practice
• Simulated radio exchanges and a persistent mistake notebook
• Morse reference, text translation and Chinese telegraph codes
• Optional microphone decoding and a local recorded-audio workbench
• Amateur-radio tools, learning statistics and local learning-material import/export
• Ten interface languages and five visual styles

Microphone permission is requested when you start live audio decoding. File selection and sharing are explicit user actions. Learning works offline. There are no accounts, ads or analytics SDKs.
