# Privacy policy

MorseCQ collects no personal data and has no account, registration, chat service, analytics or advertising. Learning history, app preferences, learning materials and imported recordings are stored on your device. The app uses microphone permission only when you start live Morse decoding and processes the audio on your device. The operating system handles files you choose to open or share.

Me > Clear learning data removes local learning data and recordings managed by MorseCQ. App preferences are kept. Uninstalling may also remove local data. Android can back up or transfer the local learning directory and app preferences, depending on system settings and limits. Android cloud backup has a 25 MB quota. The system controls iOS backup. Diagnostic logs contain error types and stack frames, omit exception messages and learning contents, stay on the device and are copied only by an explicit action in Me.

Release builds on Android ask for consent when acceptance of the current policy version is missing, including after material changes to the policy.

Effective date: 2026-11-13
