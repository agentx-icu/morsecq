# Privacy policy

MorseCQ collects no personal data and has no account, registration, chat service, analytics or advertising. Learning history, settings, materials and recordings stay on your device. Microphone permission is used only when you start live Morse decoding. Learning-material file selection, sharing and opening support pages are explicit user actions handled by the operating system. Android platform backup is disabled. Uninstalling or clearing learning data can remove local records and recordings.
