# Support

For usage or bug reports, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/agentx-icu/morsecq/issues) with your app version, platform and reproduction steps. Include learning files or recordings only when you choose to share them. Installation starts learning immediately. For Morse chat, use [DitMesh](https://github.com/agentx-icu/ditmesh).
