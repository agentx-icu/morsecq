## Contact

Open an issue at **[GitHub issues]({{ site.support_url }})** for help, bug
reports and feature requests. Issues are public: never post private messages,
backups or someone else's Tox ID there. There is no e-mail support.

## The App Store version (iOS / iPadOS)

**Is there chat on iPhone and iPad?** No. The App Store version is an offline
Morse trainer: no account, no chat, no network connection of its own. Chat is
available in the Android, macOS, Linux and Windows builds.

**Where is my progress?** On the device, in the app's private storage.
Me → Clear learning data deletes it; so does uninstalling the app.

**Decoding from the microphone does not react.** Allow microphone access in
Settings → MorseCQ, and keep the sound source close.

## Chat builds (Android, macOS, Linux, Windows)

**Do I need an account?** No. Learning works right away. Chat needs a Tox
identity, created on your device in a few seconds — no phone number or e-mail.

**Someone is bothering me.** Block them from the conversation menu, the
group's member list, the friend request or the invite. Blocked people are
listed under **Me → Blocked people**, where you can unblock them.

**Why are messages not delivered while the app is closed?** There is no
server to hold them. A message waits on your device (marked pending) and is
delivered when both of you are online.

**How do I add a friend?** Chat → contacts → share your Tox ID (text or QR
code) and add theirs. Both sides must accept.

**I lost my device. Can you restore my identity?** No — only you hold it.
Export a backup (Me → Export backup) and keep it safe.

**How do I delete my data?** Me → Delete identity, or uninstall the app. See
the [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
